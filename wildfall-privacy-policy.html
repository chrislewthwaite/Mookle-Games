Wildfall Salvagers

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

The short version

  • There are no accounts, and we never ask for your name, email address, age or location.
  • Your game progress and settings are saved on your device only. We never see them.
  • The game has optional video ads from Google. They play only when you tap a "watch an ad" button, and you never need one to play.
  • We treat every player as a child. Ads are family friendly, never personalised, and never based on what you do in other apps.
  • When you're offline, the whole game still works and nothing is sent anywhere.

For younger players

This game doesn't ask who you are and doesn't send your game anywhere. The only time it uses the internet is if you choose to watch a short advert to unlock a new look for Rook or Pip, or to give Pip a snack. You never have to watch one. If you're not sure about something, ask a parent or carer.

Who we are

Wildfall Salvagers is made by Mookle Games ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your privacy, email us at chris@mookle.org.

Information we collect

We don't collect any personal information. The game has no accounts, sign-in, chat, friends lists or in-app purchases. It doesn't ask for your name, email address, age, photos, contacts or location, and it doesn't send your gameplay to us.

Your saved game

Your expeditions, collected items, Workshop upgrades, unlocked looks and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage deletes them. If your device backs up app data to your Google account, your save may be included in that backup. The backup belongs to your Google account, and we can't access it.

Optional ads

The game shows one kind of advert: a short video you choose to watch. The "watch an ad" buttons are only in the game's Locker. There you can unlock optional looks for Rook and Pip, or buy Pip a snack. None of these rewards make the game easier. The game never shows adverts on its own, during play or when your expedition ends.

The ads are provided by Google AdMob. The game doesn't contact Google's ad service at all until you tap a "watch an ad" button. We set every ad request so that:

  • every player is treated as a child (Google's "child-directed" and "under the age of consent" settings);
  • only ads suitable for general audiences (rated G) are shown;
  • ads are not personalised, so they are not chosen from your interests or what you do in other apps;
  • the game does not give Google access to your device's advertising ID.

When the game loads or shows an ad, Google automatically receives some technical information so it can deliver the ad, count it and prevent fraud. This includes:

  • your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate a general location such as your country;
  • information about your device and the game, such as the device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • whether the ad loaded, played or was tapped, plus basic performance information;
  • an identifier that is specific to apps from the same developer (Google's "app set ID"), used for measurement and fraud prevention, not for personalised ads.

Google handles this information under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. It explains how it uses information from apps that show its ads here: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We never receive this information about you. We only see overall totals, such as how many ads were watched.

When you're offline

Wildfall Salvagers can be played completely offline. Without a connection, the ad buttons are hidden, nothing is sent and every part of the game still works. Looks you've already unlocked stay unlocked.

Google Play

You download the game through Google Play, which is covered by Google's privacy policy. If you've allowed your device to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play may show us anonymous statistics about crashes and performance. These help us fix problems, and they don't tell us who you are.

Permissions

The game uses your internet connection only to load optional ads, and it checks whether a connection is available so it can hide the ad buttons when you're offline. It can make your device vibrate for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the game's Settings. It doesn't ask for your location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Children

Wildfall Salvagers is designed to be suitable for children and follows Google Play's Families Policy. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age where you live). We treat every player as a child for advertising, as described above. If you're a parent or carer and have a question, please email us at chris@mookle.org.

Your rights

Because we don't hold any personal information about you, we have nothing to show you, correct or delete. You can delete your game data at any time by clearing the game's storage or uninstalling it. To avoid ads completely, don't tap the "watch an ad" buttons, or play offline. For information Google receives when an ad is shown, see Google's privacy policy above. You can find out about your rights there, and how to use them. If you're in the UK or the European Union, you also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. In the UK, that's the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Changes to this policy

If the game changes how it handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
