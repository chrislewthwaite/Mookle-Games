Endless, random, yours

How it plays

Explore, fight, loot, change your build, beat a guardian, go deeper. Every expedition is a fresh roll of the dice.

  1. 1

    A new world every expedition

    Wildfall never ends. Each expedition grows a fresh planet from a random seed: open spaces, winding routes, loops and forks, hidden caches, enemy camps and world events, across eight biomes that blend into each other. Turn back and it's all still there.

  2. 2

    Loot that's never the same twice

    Every weapon you find is rolled on the spot: its level, its rarity and its powers. About one ordinary drop in 2,000 is Mythic, and guardians always drop Rare or better.

  3. 3

    Build as you go

    Level up mid-run and pick one of three perk cards. Stack up to six special perks, push them to MAX, and pair them up for combos. Perks scale with your gun, so they're strong on every weapon.

  4. 4

    Grow between runs

    When a run ends, the gear stays on the planet but your Scrap comes home. Spend it in the Wayward Workshop on permanent tech across six branches, and in the Perk Lab for an extra perk card and an extra perk slot.

Powers & perks

Two ways to build. Powers come rolled on the weapons you salvage; perks are picked as you level up mid-run. Put the right ones together and things get wonderfully silly.

Weapon powers

Uncommon weapons roll 1 power, Rare 2, Epic 3, Legendary 4 and Mythic 6, each at rank I, II or III. Tech in the Workshop can push a power past what the gun rolled.

  • Sproutshot+1 projectile per rank
  • DrillbitePierces 1 more target per rank
  • Bumper RoundsRicochets to a new target within 4 m, once per rank
  • ForkseedHits split into fragments: 3 at rank I, 5 at rank III
  • Firefly GuidanceSeeking shots
  • Pollen LinkChains to 1 more target per rank
  • Party PopKills trigger bursts
  • Lucky Spark+3% crit chance and +10% crit damage per rank
  • Sizzle SapAcid damage over time that stacks up to 5 times
  • Campfire CarpetShots leave fire on the ground
  • Compost Charge10% of each hit per rank turns to corrosion
  • Fresh-Mag FizzEmpty the magazine and the reload sends out a pulse
  • Pocket OrbitGravity wells that pull enemies in
  • Firefly RocketsLaunches mini rockets: 1 per rank, up to 3
  • PipletsDeploys little Pip drones that fight with you
  • SnackbackHeals 1% of the damage you deal per rank
